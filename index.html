How to use Sound Crate

Press play, then keep shuffling until you hear something you like.

Each sound

  • 🎲 New sound swaps in a different file.
  • Lock keeps that sound when you shuffle everything.
  • Mute turns it off. Solo plays only the sounds marked Solo.
  • Double-click the left-or-right slider to put it back in the middle.

The whole song

  • Speed makes everything faster or slower.
  • Key tunes every sound to the same notes. Major keys sound happy, minor keys sound moody.
  • Loop is how long the pattern is before it repeats.

Shortcuts

Record a sound lets you choose a laptop or USB microphone, record a take, preview it, and add it to a slot or the collection. Use Save original WAV to keep the raw recording.

Using your loops in GarageBand

Save project saves your chosen sounds, exact loops, and settings as a file. Saved sessions keeps named mixes on this computer. Use Open project to load a file. Load the original folders for their full collection.

In the desktop app, drag a sound's Save tab or Drag stems to DAW into your music app. In the browser, use Export stems, then drag the downloaded WAV files from Finder.

Adjust offers gain, half/double time, octave, a 16-step pattern, and a four-bar repeat. New slice changes the section of the same file. ★ favors a sound in safe shuffles. Hype adds gentle bass and treble boosts.
