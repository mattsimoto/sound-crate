Turn your forgotten sounds into a beat

Pick a folder with sounds in it. Sound Crate listens to every file, then stretches and tunes them so they all play together at the same speed and in the same key.

You can also drop a folder onto this window. Good places to look: Music, Downloads and GarageBand. Your sounds stay on your Mac.

How to use Sound Crate

Press play, then keep shuffling until you hear something you like.

Each sound

  • 🎲 New sound swaps in a different file.
  • Lock keeps that sound when you shuffle everything.
  • Mute turns it off. Solo plays only the sounds marked Solo.
  • Double-click the left-or-right slider to put it back in the middle.

The whole song

  • Speed makes everything faster or slower.
  • Key tunes every sound to the same notes. Major keys sound happy, minor keys sound moody.
  • Loop is how long the pattern is before it repeats.

Shortcuts

Space play or stop   R shuffle everything

Using your loops in GarageBand

In Chrome, drag the Save tab on any sound straight into GarageBand. In Safari, click Save each sound, then drag the files from your Downloads folder into GarageBand.
