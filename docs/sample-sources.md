# Recorded horn and brass sources

The built-in brass pack is synthesized. These external libraries provide other sounds to download and import. They are not bundled with Sound Crate.

| Source | What to look for | Published license / terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Trumpet melody — trumpetplayer](https://freesound.org/people/trumpetplayer/sounds/651203/) | A solo trumpet melody, about 30 seconds | CC0; original download requires a Freesound login |
| [French horn glissandi — PhonosUPF](https://freesound.org/people/PhonosUPF/sounds/490176/) | French horn gestures | CC0 |
| [Trumpet frullato — PhonosUPF](https://freesound.org/people/PhonosUPF/sounds/503154/) | Flutter-tongue trumpet texture | CC0 |
| [Philharmonia sound samples](https://philharmonia.co.uk/resources/sound-samples/) | Trumpet, French horn, trombone, and tuba notes | Music use, including commercial works, is allowed; redistribution as raw samples or a sampler instrument is restricted |

Use **Add sound → Import files** after downloading. Give single notes a role, then try them on Pads. Tempo and key estimates may need manual correction for expressive phrases and unmetered playing.

The app's procedural packs are original synthesized material. No recordings from these sources have been copied into the app. Source terms checked October 2, 2026.
